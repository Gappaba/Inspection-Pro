Inspection Pro v105

Minnesota MIP measurement checks use the supplied Minnesota State Patrol
handbook. The app flags a confirmed failure on its matching checklist
item and uses the existing discrepancy and photo workflow. Failures
found in the Page 2 questions are routed for documentation before review
or signing. The inspector still checks the rest of each item.

Checks included
- Tread depth below 4/32 inch on a power-unit steering axle or below
  2/32 inch on other axles. Exactly 4/32 and 2/32 meet those limits.
- Tire pressure at or below half of the entered cold rated pressure.
- Air brake stroke over the limit for the selected handbook chamber
  designation with recorded reservoir pressure from 90 to 100 PSI.
- Fifth-wheel movement over the applicable 3/8 or 1/2 inch limit.
- Tractor protection airflow stopping below 20 PSI, or an observed
  glad-hand leak during the service-brake test.
- Steering free play above the handbook table limit for the selected
  steering system and recorded wheel diameter.
- Surge-brake trailer GVWR above the handbook maximum or applicable
  towing vehicle ratio.

For a new Minnesota power-unit inspection, select the axle type before
tire measurement so the steering limit applies to the correct axle.
For a prior draft without an axle type, verify the applicable tread
limit manually. Choose manual verification when a chamber or steering
diameter falls outside the handbook tables or a different manufacturer
specification applies. If a reference tire pressure is unavailable,
verify inflation manually. The Federal workflow retains its prior rules.

Open a sample inspection to verify the prompts and discrepancy routing
