Inspection Pro v107

Federal inspection workflow and record
1. Create a separate report for each inspected vehicle or trailer. Enter
   the operating motor carrier or intermodal equipment provider, date,
   inspector name, and a vehicle identifier.
2. Configure the axles and brake systems on the unit. The Federal
   checklist includes detailed brake items for the selected configuration
   along with all 15 Appendix A inspection categories. Use the guide in
   each section for rejection criteria. Use N/A where the equipment or
   requirement does not apply to the unit.
3. Complete the checklist, document failures, and draw the inspector
   signature. Finalization checks for outstanding checklist items and
   missing report identification.
4. Save, share or print the finalized PDF. It contains a landscape Letter
   summary, captured signature and continuation pages with individual
   checklist results and complete findings. The full in-app report also
   shows the recorded brake and tire measurements where applicable.

Sources
49 CFR 396.17, 396.19, 396.21 and Appendix A to Part 396
https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-396

No database migration is needed. Existing Federal drafts can continue
after the new required report fields and checklist items are completed.
