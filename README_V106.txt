Inspection Pro v106

Federal periodic inspections now flag measured failures under 49 CFR
Part 396 Appendix A and the indicated Part 393 requirements. A confirmed
failure opens the existing discrepancy and photo workflow before signing.
The inspector still assesses the other conditions on each checklist item.

Federal checks
- Tread below 4/32 in. on a power-unit steering axle or below 2/32 in.
  elsewhere. Choose each axle type when setting up a power unit. Inspect
  flat tires and leaks manually. The Minnesota cold-pressure rule stays
  in the Minnesota MIP workflow.
- Pushrod stroke above the applicable limit at 80–90 PSI. For an unlisted
  actuator, select and record its marked readjustment limit or, when only
  rated stroke is marked, the rated stroke used for the 80% limit.
- Fifth-wheel pivot and slider movement over 3/8 in. or upper/lower
  horizontal movement over 1/2 in.
- Steering lash above the recorded diameter and steering-system limit
  for the listed 16, 18, 20, and 22 in. wheels, and nonrotational linkage
  movement over 1/4 in. Verify other diameters manually.
- Low-air warning activation below the lesser of 55 PSI or half the
  governor cut-out pressure, tractor protection operation outside
  20–45 PSI on an applicable air-braked towing unit, hydraulic master
  cylinder fill below 1/4, and surge-brake trailer GVWR limits.

Record only applicable measurements. Use manual inspection if the
reference value or required test conditions cannot be established.
The v105 Minnesota checks are included. No database migration is needed.
